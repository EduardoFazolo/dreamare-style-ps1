User refs (docs/research/ps1-ps2-refs/user/ref1-10, + fan-wiki game shots in docs/research/ps1-ps2-refs): PS1 Hagrid, GoldenEye N64 guards/scientists/Xenia, Majora's Mask moon. Common thread: a REAL PHOTOGRAPH of a face slapped flat on a blocky faceted head, boxy low-poly body, blurry low-res cloth photos, stiff A-pose, uncanny because the face is too real for the body.
User's method video: "YOURSELF as a PS1 Style Character | Blender Beginner Tutorial" (youtube OjiwRIE21UA): photograph a real person front/side/back/top, model a round-cube head (very low poly) to match front+side, project the photos as texture.

**Why:** concept-art prompts ("character model sheet, flat studio light") make illustrations with ink outlines; that style survives the bake.
**How to apply:** design inputs = realistic PHOTOS of a person (Klein is good at photos; [[photo-character-recipe]]), not "model sheet"/"in game" art; build at a PS1 budget (few hundred tris, faceted, no smoothing), face = the photo projected flat. Style-reference tool: ai.py image --refs (tools/comfy/gen_ref.py).
