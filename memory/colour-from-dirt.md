2026-10-26, an isolated room: user said "when I told you there is not enough color, I didn't tell you to add random fucking lights".
The bar is Silent Hill 3/4 rooms + SH street at night (refs: stained mottled wallpaper, cracked plaster with grime and
rust, cracked grimy tile floors, moldy ceilings, one practical light (hanging bulb / flashlight) with fast falloff and
dark corners, period clutter: iron bed, IV stand, cabinet+TV, chair with book, framed butterflies, patterned rug).
"so fucking nice, chill, creepy and nostalgic".

**Why:** colour must live IN the surfaces (stains, rust, mould, paper patterns), lit by one warm practical; coloured
halos/tube lights read as random and cheap.
**How to apply:** enclosed rooms = dirty textured walls/floor/ceiling + one practical + clutter + dark corners; no
coloured halo lights unless the story needs one. Also: empty slides that last long get cut shorter. See [[no-flat-colors]],
[[environment-beauty-checklist]].
